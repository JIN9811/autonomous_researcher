6. Recording

Voice Speed -35
Recording voice cues
  • Recording episode N: episode recording has started.
  • Reset the environment: recording is paused for the reset interval before the next episode.
  • Re-record episode: the current episode was rejected and will be recorded again.
  • Stop recording: recording loop is closing and devices will disconnect.
  • Exiting: LeRobot recording command has finished.
Piper uses the packaged English en_US-lessac-medium neural voice. Voice Engine selects the TTS backend; Voice Speed is auto-saved and applied when recording starts.

Live controls map to LeRobot recording keys: Save/Next sends Right Arrow, Retry sends Left Arrow, Finish sends Esc. Force Stop performs emergency cleanup of LeRobot live processes if a session or stale subprocess keeps devices open.
