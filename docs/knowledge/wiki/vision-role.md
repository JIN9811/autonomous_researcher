# Vision Agent

Vision reviews a bounded observation contract using existing detector results and multimodal image evidence. It produces vision_signal.v1 and role-specific verification evidence for the current graph consumer. ActiveCam capture ordering, freshness and detector gates remain code-owned. An image or Wiki citation cannot establish physical safety or authorize another domain task. Device acquisition and movement remain with registered tools and bridges.
