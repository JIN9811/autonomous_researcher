# Lab Equipment Agent

Lab Equipment evaluates suitability of an approved stacked Flow, then reviews terminal evidence and bounded recovery choices. It does not interrupt a running Flow with repeated model decisions. Existing Flow order, Skills, method settings and worker payloads remain immutable to the model. Successful work must not be repeated. Verified CSV and readiness feed Manipulation clearance, fresh Vision verification and then Analysis. Desktop and instrument input remain with registered workers and tools.
